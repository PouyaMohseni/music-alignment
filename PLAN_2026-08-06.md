# Plan: beating cyolo_sb on real audio (2026-08-06)

Synthesised from a 15-agent sweep (6 evidence/literature, 5 designs, 3 adversarial
lenses, 1 completeness critic). The synthesis agent itself died on a session
limit; this document is written from its inputs, with every load-bearing claim
re-verified by hand. Claims I checked and *rejected* are marked.

---

## 1. Verdict on the target

**The bar is 79.9, not 63.0 — verified in-house, not just from the paper.**
`results/.../cyolo_rp_check-142667.log` runs the released checkpoints on our own
MSMD-Rec copy:

| in-house | @0.05 | @0.1 | **@0.5** | @1.0 | @5.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cyolo room | 56.3 | 58.1 | **71.2** | 74.9 | 91.9 |
| **cyolo_sb room** | 61.0 | **63.0** | **79.9** | 83.2 | 96.0 |
| cyolo_sb rp_synth | 76.3 | 78.2 | **87.2** | 89.4 | 98.7 |

`63.0` is the ≤0.1 s cell. Our metric is ≤0.5 s. The gap from R3 (45.6) is
**+34.3**, not +17.

**Is 70+ reachable without the unreleased "+A" data? Yes, demonstrably** — both
`cyolo` (71.2) and `cyolo_sb` (79.9) were trained on exactly the data we have.
But the caveat is family-shaped and sobering: **no non-detection model has ever
exceeded 58.5 on this tier** (MM-Loc 58.5, RL 47.6, CUNet 22.4).

**Can 25 pages even resolve this? Mostly no.** A 20,000-replicate piece-level
cluster bootstrap gives R3 room = 45.4 with **sd = 10.1**, 95% CI [29.2, 64.7].
Naive binomial sd is 0.75, so the design effect is ~180× and the effective
sample size is ~25, not 4415. One Chopin nocturne is 29.8% of all room onsets.

Consequences we must act on:
- **Our internal ranking is noise.** R3 45.6 / N3 44.7 / MERT_B2 43.7 / R2a 42.3
  all sit inside one spread. R2a's "+3.8" and N2's "−2.6" are probably not real
  effects, and I reported both as if they were.
- **Paired comparisons survive.** cyolo_sb − R3 = **+34.6 [+22.9, +43.4]** is far
  outside noise. So the *gap* is real even though our internal ordering is not.
- Combined recipe-vs-recipe sd (bootstrap + training seed) ≈ **4.3**. Any claimed
  win under ~10 points needs ≥4 seeds or it is undefendable.

## 2. The decisive decomposition (new, and it reframes everything)

Per-piece, onset-weighted, on the identical 25 pages. EASY-16 = 16 pages every
MERT model handles; HARD-9 = 6 Chopin op9 nocturne pages + Schumann op68-01/-06/-08.

| model | ALL | EASY-16 | HARD-9 |
|---|---|---|---|
| cyolo_sb_a | 86.5 | 92.1 | 78.1 |
| **cyolo_sb** | **79.9** | **87.7** | **68.5** |
| cyolo | 71.2 | 85.3 | 50.4 |
| **R3 (ours)** | **45.4** | **64.7** | **17.0** |

Three things follow, and each kills a hypothesis I have been carrying:

1. **The HARD-9 is not unlearnable.** cyolo_sb gets 68.5 where our best-ever is
   22.3. So "repeat structure makes those pages impossible" and "the ground
   truth is broken there" are both dead. Three of the five designs listed one of
   these as a live risk.
2. **The EASY-16 is not saturated either.** 87.7 vs our 64.7. Decomposed,
   0.595×(87.7−64.7) = 13.7 points of the gap is EASY-16 and 0.405×(68.5−17.0) =
   20.9 is HARD-9. **EASY-16 is 40% of the deficit, not ~0%.** All four
   architects assumed otherwise.
3. **The HARD-9 mechanism is identified and measured.** cyolo_sb beats cyolo by
   **+18.1 on HARD-9** but only **+2.4 on EASY-16** — with augmentation held
   constant by the authors' own statement. The famous "+8.7 from SB" is almost
   entirely a HARD-9 effect, i.e. it targets exactly our persistent
   mis-registration failure and nothing else.

## 3. Claims I previously made that did not survive

- **"MM-Loc vs CUNet = 36 points from output parameterisation" — overstated.**
  A decode ablation on the worst-affected model (`decode_abl-142784.log`) shows
  removing the empty-mask pathology entirely buys **+2.6** (21.5 → 24.1), and the
  best decode variant reaches 26.4, i.e. **+4.9 maximum**. B1a's empty-mask rate
  on room is already 4.0% vs pretrained CB_TA's 24.5% — our MERT models have
  *already captured* most of what a decode fix could give. The MM-Loc gap is
  real but is not a free +36 available to us via re-parameterisation.
  This is why RIBBON-MG is not the primary bet despite being elegant.
- **"CYOLO degrades 4.3 vs our 45" — only true of the non-reproducible +A model.**
  Reproducibly: cyolo −17.3, cyolo_sb −9.4 (and only −7.3 on matched rp_synth pages).
- **"cyolo_sb uses a multi-scale FPN" — wrong.** It is three anchor+class heads on
  ONE 52×52 P3 map. That makes SB *cheaper* to port than I claimed, not harder.
- **"MERT sees an 8.8 dB scale mismatch" (agent claim) — rejected.** Measured on
  matched pages: rp_synth 0.0332 RMS, room 0.0316 (−0.4 dB), di-left 0.0303
  (−0.8 dB). Under 1 dB. The missing `do_normalize` is still a real deviation
  from MERT's documented usage and should be fixed, but it is not a large
  train/test scale shift.
- **B6 was never fairly tested.** `extensions/augmentation/impulse_response.py:85`
  uses `fftconvolve(..., mode='same')`, shifting audio ~half an IR length against
  its own onset labels. "IR augmentation scored 15.6 and came last" is not
  evidence about IR augmentation. (R2's bank uses `mode='full'` and is fine.)

## 4. The diagnostic gate — run before funding anything

**G1. rp_synth control — LAUNCHED (jobs 288617–288628).** The same 25 pages, same
score images, same GT, training-soundfont audio. Never evaluated by any of our
models. Splits our deficit into acoustics vs page difficulty, which no existing
number can do (our only synthetic reference is 125 *different* pages).
Calibration: cyolo_sb_a is 88.6 rp_synth / 86.5 room — only −2.1 on identical pages.
- If HARD-9 rp_synth ≈ 85 and room ≈ 17 → the deficit is **acoustic**; audio-side
  designs are correctly aimed.
- If HARD-9 rp_synth ≈ 30 → the deficit is **structural**; every audio-side design
  is mis-aimed and multi-granularity supervision becomes the only live idea.

**G2. Top-p heatmap-mass oracle (CPU, free).** Per onset frame, is the true x inside
the top-1/5/20% of the heatmap's x-marginal mass? Split EASY-16/HARD-9. This
simultaneously upper-bounds *every* decode, prior, filter and tracker idea on the
slate. If HARD-9 top-5% recall is under ~40%, no temporal mechanism can recover
those frames and the effort must move to supervision/representation.

**G3. Evaluate our own cyolo_sb repro on all three tiers.** The checkpoint exists
(`/scratch/pmohseni/cyolo_repro/.../best_model.pt`, job 89565 TIMEOUT at 24 h,
epoch ~20/50). **It was launched without `--ir_path`**, so it lacks the IR
augmentation the paper credits for room robustness. If it lands >5 points below
79.9, relaunch with a real IR bank before building anything on top of it.

**Protocol fix, applies from now on:** every room number reported as a piece-level
cluster-bootstrap mean with 95% CI *and* the paired delta vs its named
comparator; EASY-16/HARD-9 split always shown; ≥4 seeds for any claimed delta
under 10 points.

## 5. The primary bet — DUET-SB

Ranked **#1 unanimously** by all three adversarial lenses (evidence 8, ceiling 9,
feasibility 8), for one reason that no other design shares: **its floor is the bar.**

Warm-start from our own trained cyolo_sb and bolt a zero-init-gated MERT
side-channel onto the conditioning vector. At step 0 the network is bit-identical
to cyolo_sb, so the design starts at ~79.9 rather than at our 45.6. Every added
component is gated or ablatable at eval.

**The seam.** `models/yolo.py:107` is
`forward = predict(score, encode_sequence(compute_spec(perf))[0])`. Inside
`conditioning_networks.py:100-133`, `encode_samples` chunks the spectrogram to
(n,1,40,78) → `enc` → 32-d, and z = `z_enc([h_last(64), last_steps(32)])`. That
32-d chunk embedding is the cheapest place in either system to inject a second
modality. `self.enc` stays byte-identical so the warm start loads; add
`mert_enc`, widen `seq_model` to LSTM(64,64) and `z_enc` to Linear(128,128).

**Its own falsifier, and it is cheap:** run a top-K objectness oracle on the
*released* cyolo_sb over room — best-of-16 candidates per onset frame. If that
oracle gains <+3 over argmax, the correct position is not in the candidate set on
failing frames, and the tracker term carrying most of the predicted margin is
unreachable by construction. One CPU pass kills or confirms the largest term.

**Known weaknesses to design around:** the LSTM ticks once per 40 frames (2.0 s),
so MERT's frame-level detail is pooled away before recurrence sees it; and
precomputed MERT cannot see on-the-fly augmentation, so the MERT branch risks
being the *less* augmented one.

## 6. Grafts (fold in, in this order)

1. **SB multi-level heads onto our own R3 recipe** — promoted from "component of
   RIBBON-MG" to a standalone arm. Highest evidence-per-GPU-hour on the slate:
   the mechanism is measured at +18.1 on exactly our failing subset, and the
   geometry needs no data we lack. Primary readout is the HARD-9 split.
2. **Distillation from the released cyolo_sb teacher.** *Nobody proposed this.* A
   verified teacher scoring 68.5 on HARD-9 (vs our 22.3) is already loaded
   in-repo, and we have unlimited unlabelled synthetic + degraded audio to distil
   on. It also directly measures how much of CYOLO's advantage is transferable
   into a dense-heatmap student versus intrinsic to detection.
3. **Degraded validation tier for checkpoint selection.** Synthetic-val correlates
   with room at **+0.159 Pearson — statistically zero**, and three of four designs
   select on it. We have been early-stopping on a signal orthogonal to the
   objective. The degradation chain already exists and is correct.
4. **Test-time augmentation** — average the x-marginal over 3–5 perturbed copies.
   Zero retraining, composes with everything.
5. **PEFT on MERT (LoRA / top-2 layers).** All four designs freeze MERT; no
   experiment in 22 has ever tested that assumption, and MERT's clean-tier
   deficit (di-left 55.2 vs CBEncoder 64.5) is the classic frozen-feature symptom.

## 7. What NOT to build

- **ARC-1 (ASAP corpus) — killed by 2 of 3 lenses.** No working MusicXML →
  pixel-accurate notehead toolchain here (no lilypond/musicxml2ly on PATH, MSMD's
  coordinate-emitting backend not reconstructible without root), and ASAP ships
  **zero audio**. Failure mode is corrupted training labels, not a crash.
- **RIBBON-MG as a full system** — its ceiling argument is refuted by our own
  decode ablation (§3). Keep only its multi-granularity supervision, which is
  graft #1.
- **More R1 normalisation variants.** Measured at +1.2 / −7.0 / −12.1 / +1.5 /
  −2.7. Done.
- **Output-level ensembling of our 19 models** — oracle cap is 54.3.

## 9. Honest ceiling

- **Floor-inheriting path (DUET-SB): 79.9 by construction, realistically 80–86**
  if the MERT side-channel and tracker each contribute at half their discounted
  estimates. This is the only path with a credible claim above cyolo_sb.
- **Our own CB_TA family: realistically 55–70.** Grafts 1–4 plausibly take R3
  from 45.6 to ~55–65. Reaching 79.9 within the dense-heatmap family would mean
  beating every non-detection model ever published on this tier by 20+ points.
- **If the primary bet only half-works**, the defensible paper is not "we beat
  cyolo_sb" but "we more than doubled the published CUNet on real audio (22.4 →
  ~55-65) and identified the mechanism", which is a real result and is already
  half-secured.
- **Caveat on all of the above:** with piece-level sd ≈ 10 on 25 pages, a
  5-point difference is not measurable. Building a larger real-audio test set is
  the single highest-value non-modelling investment available, and nobody
  proposed it.
